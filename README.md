# Bike Sharing Demand Analysis: From Data to Statistical Evidence

## Project Overview
This project evaluates temporal, seasonal, and environmental patterns in urban bike-sharing demand using exploratory data analysis (EDA) and classical statistical inference. Operating as part of the bike-sharing analytics team, we investigate two years of hourly rental logs (2011–2012) from the Capital Bikeshare system to answer the central operational question:

> **When and under what conditions does bike-sharing demand change?**

We formulate research questions, verify underlying assumptions, separate statistical significance from operational magnitude, and translate our quantitative findings into actionable fleet management and dock rebalancing recommendations[cite: 1, 4, 5].
